description: "Planned products built on the same rToken standard."
---

<Warning>
  These modules are planned for Stage 3 (indicatively 2029 to 2030) and are not available. They are described to show how the rToken standard extends.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Liquidity Vaults">
    Deposit rTokens and borrow stablecoins against the most liquid, independently valued assets, while continuing to receive the asset's net income. Launch parameters use conservative loan-to-value ratios. Liquidations run as on-chain Dutch auctions restricted to attested bidders and designated liquidity providers, with valuation oracles fed by independent appraisals and venue prices with haircuts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Auto-Compounding Vaults">
    Solana's micro-fees allow income to be distributed continuously rather than quarterly. Investors can opt in to a vault that reinvests distributions into additional fractions of the same or chosen rTokens. The vault issues its own share, and the underlying rToken remains strictly 1:1. Reinvestment runs on the investor's standing, non-discretionary instruction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI agents">
    Issuer-side underwriting agents inside the SPV Factory (document parsing, lease and loan-tape abstraction, title and charge cross-checks, valuation consistency checks) with a human underwriter accountable for every listing. Non-custodial investor agents that collect distributions, rebalance risk and execute standing strategies within investor-set limits, acting through scoped session keys and smart-wallet spending limits. They never hold the investor's keys, and discretionary portfolio management is offered only through an appropriately licensed entity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Partner distribution and US retail access">
    rTokens distributed through external apps, and access for US retail through partner structures such as Regulation A+ via a broker-dealer or portal and a transfer agent.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

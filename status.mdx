Rivestor is built in three stages. Each stage ends at a defined exit gate and builds on the same rToken standard.

## Where Rivestor is today

<Check>
  **Stage 1, marketplace in private beta (MVP V1).** The marketplace is built and opening to a limited number of users through a waitlist, limited log-ins and pre-deposits. This tests demand and supply with exclusive assets before the protocol goes live.
</Check>

<Note>
  There is **no public REST API** at this stage. Integration happens through the RTCS programs on Solana and, from Stage 2, the Embedded SDK. See [Embedded SDK](/protocol/embedded-sdk).
</Note>

## Stages

| Stage | Purpose | What ships | Indicative timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Marketplace and liquidity** | Test demand and supply with exclusive assets | Private beta, waitlist, limited log-ins, pre-deposits; wrapping of already on-chain assets (Engine B); first Engine A pilot series | Q4 2026 to Q2 2027 |
| **2. RTCS for any asset** | Fractional ownership and investing | Full SPV Factory for real estate, private credit and commodities; global wrapping of third-party issuers; secondary trading for eligible investors; fiat on- and off-ramp; Embedded SDK beta | H2 2027 to 2028 |
| **3. RWA ecosystem** | Make the rToken the standard | Partner widgets, Liquidity Vaults, Auto-Compounding Vaults, AI agents, own venue licences, access for US retail through partner structures | 2029 to 2030 |

Timing is indicative and depends on audits, licensing and market conditions.

## Gates before mainnet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Independent audits">
    The RTCS Vault, Compliance Hook, Fee Router and Distribution programs are audited by at least two independent firms before they move from devnet to mainnet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verifiable builds">
    Programs are deployed from verifiable builds so anyone can check that the on-chain code matches the audited source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staged exposure">
    New vaults run under a TVL cap during their first months. Improvements ship as new vault versions that holders opt into.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Features marked **Stage 2** or **Stage 3** in these docs describe the target design and are not yet available.
</Warning>

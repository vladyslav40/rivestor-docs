title: "Regulatory approach"
description: "A non-custodial design and licensed partners keep the regulatory surface small at launch."

Rivestor is a technology provider, not a fund manager. Regulated steps are handled by licensed partners at launch, and Rivestor adds its own licences as the business expands.

## Five principles

| Principle | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| No custody | Rivestor never holds user funds. Investors use self-custodial wallets. |
| Compliance in code | KYC/AML, whitelists and geographic restrictions are enforced on-chain by RTCS, so only verified users can transact. Contracts are open and auditable by anyone. |
| No advice, no guarantees | Rivestor shows risk metrics and on-chain data only. Issuers set the terms. |
| Licensed partners first | Fiat on- and off-ramp, KYC/AML and custody are handled by licensed partners. |
| Own licences as we scale | Tokenization does not remove licensing duties. Rivestor will obtain permissions such as FCA and FSRA authorization as the model expands. |

## Licensing roadmap

| Stage | Regulatory mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage 1** | Rivestor Ltd as technology provider. Arranging and promotion through an FCA-authorized principal. Trading of wrapped assets through a licensed partner venue. Reg S (non-US) and Reg D (accredited US) only. First Engine A pilot in a Delaware Registered Series. |
| **Stage 2** | SEC-registered transfer agent and partner broker-dealer or ATS (US). FSRA application for Rivestor Markets (ADGM) via the RegLab. Continued UK Appointed Representative coverage. |
| **Stage 3** | Own FSRA venue licence. US ATS or broker-dealer (own or acquired). UK DSS hybrid DSD and MTF or full authorization. EU DLT MTF under the DLT Pilot Regime. |

## Classification of wrapped tokens

Because a Tier 1 or Tier 2 rToken may be characterized as a separate instrument comparable to a depositary receipt, such rTokens are listed only through the licensed venue entity or partner, which takes listing and disclosure responsibility. Every listing shows its tier.

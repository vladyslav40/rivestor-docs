A basic token can be deployed in minutes. The harder problem is the gap between the on-chain ledger and off-chain ownership of title, and the fragmentation of the market itself. Rivestor addresses it with three inseparable layers.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Layer 1: Legal isolation (SPV)" icon="landmark">
    The asset and 100% of the equity move into a bankruptcy-remote SPV series, with an independent Security Trustee holding the Golden Share. The rToken mint address is the statutory register of members.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Layer 2: Financial control" icon="vault">
    A title protection package registered in favour of the Security Trustee, a Lockbox account and an irrevocable Notice of Direction, with automated distribution on Solana.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Layer 3: On-chain compliance (RTCS)" icon="shield-check">
    Token-2022 with the Compliance Hook enforces KYC/AML attestations, jurisdiction, investor count and lock-up rules on every transfer. Permanent Delegate and Freeze support court-ordered recovery.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Which layers apply

| Layer | Engine A (native) | Engine B (wrapped) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Legal isolation | Applied in full | The issuer's responsibility, verified through the RTCS Integration Standard before wrapping |
| 2. Financial control | Applied in full | The issuer's responsibility, verified through the Integration Standard |
| 3. On-chain compliance | Applies | Applies without exception |

## The exploitation scenario

The case the structure is designed to close:

<Warning>
  The owner of a commercial building tokenizes a 10% interest through a generic smart contract. Without legal isolation, the owner keeps 100% operational control over the owning company and the building, while the other 90% of the equity sits off-chain in an ordinary share register. In time, the owner covertly sells a 60% interest to an offshore company or takes a bank loan secured against the property. The token contract knows nothing of this and keeps displaying "ownership", while the bank already holds a first-ranking charge. Such a token is a **synthetic obligation** that depends on the owner's good faith.
</Warning>

Rivestor closes the gap with the **whole-equity rule** (100% of the SPV's equity is on the register), the **Golden Share** (no sale or charge without the trustee's consent) and a **title protection package**. See [SPV structure](/legal/spv-structure) and [Title protection and Lockbox](/legal/title-protection).

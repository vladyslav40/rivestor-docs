The **Security Trustee** is an independent, licensed fiduciary that is not affiliated with Rivestor. It holds the Golden Share, the title protection package and the keys that co-sign extraordinary on-chain actions.

## What the trustee controls

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Corporate consent" icon="stamp">
    Without the Golden Share's consent the SPV cannot sell or charge the asset, amend its constitution, issue new interests, merge, or file for insolvency.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Title and cash" icon="vault">
    Holds the title restriction, the first-ranking charge under the Investor Protection Deed and the Lockbox account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="On-chain authority" icon="key-round">
    Co-signs mint authority release and metadata updates. With the Compliance Committee, controls Permanent Delegate and Freeze Authority. Holds time-limited Pausable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Replacement rights" icon="refresh-cw">
    Holds the right to replace a property manager on a holder resolution.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Trustee models by listing track

| Track | Trustee model |
| --- | --- |
| Express | One programme trustee covers every Express series |
| Standard | Programme trustee with a Golden Share per series |
| Institutional | Dedicated trustee per asset |

## Why independence matters

If Rivestor held these powers, a failure of Rivestor would be a failure of investor protection. Placing them with an independent trustee means no single Rivestor key can move, mint, freeze or burn any rToken, and the legal register can be reconstructed from the Registrar's Golden Record.

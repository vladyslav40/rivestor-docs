## Cash-flow control

<Steps>
  <Step title="Notice of Direction">
    All tenants (or, for private credit, all obligors) sign an irrevocable Notice of Direction to remit payments exclusively to a Lockbox account controlled by the Security Trustee, bypassing the former owner and the property manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Operating waterfall">
    Operating expenses are released from the Lockbox under an agreed waterfall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automated distribution">
    The trustee's banking rail converts receipts into USDC through a regulated on-ramp and funds the RTCS Distribution program, which pays holders pro rata against a record-date snapshot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Title Protection Package

An equity holder cannot be secured by a mortgage over its own company's property, because a charge must secure an obligation. Rivestor therefore registers a three-part package.

| Part | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Restriction or consent requirement | A restriction on the title register in favour of the Security Trustee. In England and Wales, a Land Registry restriction requiring the trustee's written consent to any disposition. |
| 2. First-ranking legal charge | A charge in favour of the Security Trustee securing the SPV's obligations under an **Investor Protection Deed**: no disposal, no encumbrance, distribution waterfall, and a liquidated-damages claim equal to net asset value on breach. |
| 3. Negative pledge | A negative pledge in the SPV's constitution. |

For private-credit assets, the Security Trustee holds a true first-lien security interest over the borrower's collateral for the benefit of rToken holders.

## Alignment of fees and taxes

- The platform's management fee is subordinated to investor distributions.
- Where required, a tax withholding module in the Distribution program applies withholding using the holder's tax-status attestation and routes the amount to the paying agent.

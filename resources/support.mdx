## Who to contact for what

| You are | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| An investor in the private beta | [Getting started](/investors/getting-started), then email us |
| An issuer or asset owner | [Issuers overview](/issuers/overview), then email us |
| A wallet, fund or app integrating rTokens | [Embedded SDK](/protocol/embedded-sdk), then email us |

<Tip>
  Include your wallet address (never your private keys, seed phrase or verification codes) and a short description of your question.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Rivestor staff never ask for your seed phrase, private keys or verification codes.
</Warning>

A portfolio groups investments you manage together. Create separate portfolios for separate goals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a portfolio">
    Select **New portfolio** and give it a name that describes its goal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allocate funds">
    Choose how much of your balance to put in this portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the summary and confirm. You can review performance at any time from your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The value of investments can go down as well as up. You may get back less than you invest.
</Warning>

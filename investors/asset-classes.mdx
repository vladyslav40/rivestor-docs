Rivestor focuses on real-value assets, starting with exclusive assets that retail investors cannot reach elsewhere. Rivestor does not list tokenized stocks, bonds or T-bills.

| Asset class | What it is | Risk profile | Typical ticket |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Real estate | Fractional shares in income-producing property | Medium to high | $50 to $1,000 |
| Private credit | Lending backed by invoices, SME loans or real estate debt | Medium | $100 to $10,000 |
| Commodities | Exposure to gold and other commodities. Price-driven, no income | Medium | $100 and above |

<Note>
  Ticket sizes are indicative and set per listing. Issuers set terms and expected returns. Rivestor does not set or guarantee returns.
</Note>

## What every listing shows

<Steps>
  <Step title="Engine and tier">
    Whether the asset is natively issued (Engine A) or wrapped (Engine B, with its integration tier).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Legal references">
    The legal register reference and SHA-256 hashes of the constitutional documents, valuation and leases, embedded in the rToken metadata.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Risk metrics">
    On-chain data and risk metrics. Rivestor presents data and does not give advice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the structures behind each class, see [SPV structure](/legal/spv-structure) and [listing tracks](/issuers/listing-tracks).

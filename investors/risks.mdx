---

<Warning>
  The value of an investment can go down as well as up, and you may lose some or all of the capital you invest. Read this page together with the [disclaimer](/legal/disclaimer).
</Warning>

| Risk | What it means for you | Mitigation in the design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asset quality | An issuer may underperform or default. | Due diligence on every issuer, transparent risk metrics and no promised returns. |
| Title divergence | The asset could be sold, charged or diluted outside the register. | Whole-equity rule, Golden Share, title-register restriction, first-ranking charge and negative pledge. |
| Operational | A property manager or tenant stops paying. | Bankruptcy-remote series, Lockbox that bypasses the manager, trustee's right to replace the manager. |
| Third-party tokens (Engine B) | A wrapped issuer's token fails or has a legal defect. | Tiered integration standard shown on every listing, immutable vaults with TVL caps for Tiers 1 and 2. Rivestor does not guarantee the original. |
| Smart-contract security | A flaw in the programs puts assets at risk. | Two independent audits, verifiable builds, open contracts, timelocked upgrades, Pausable extension. |
| Key loss | You lose your wallet keys. | Permanent Delegate can burn and reissue under Security Trustee and Compliance Committee multisig, on verified identity via the Registrar. |
| Liquidity | Early secondary markets may have low activity. | Concentrated liquidity from wrapped assets, designated market makers and liquidity reserves. |
| Regulation | Rules differ by country and change. | Non-custodial design, licensed partners for regulated steps, jurisdiction rules enforced by the hook. |
| Network | A Solana outage or consensus upgrade incident. | No trade is legally settled before finalized commitment. The venue halts automatically on stalled finality. |

See the full [risk matrix](/legal/risk-matrix).

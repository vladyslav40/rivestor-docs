Rivestor is non-custodial. You hold rTokens in your own Solana wallet, and eligibility is attested on-chain rather than stored in a Rivestor account.

<Note>
  The marketplace is in **private beta**. Access is limited and granted through a waitlist. The full fractional marketplace on RTCS is Stage 2. See [Status and roadmap](/status).
</Note>

## What you need

- A self-custodial Solana wallet
- A government-issued ID for identity verification
- Eligibility for the pool you want to join (see [Jurisdiction rules](#jurisdiction-rules))

## Onboarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the waitlist or accept an invitation">
    Private beta access is granted in limited batches. Pre-deposits from early users help test demand before launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity once">
    KYC and AML checks run through a provider such as Sumsub. When you pass, an approved credential issuer writes an attestation to the Solana Attestation Service (SAS) for your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your token account is unlocked">
    New rToken accounts are created frozen. Once your wallet holds a valid attestation, the RTCS KYC Authority thaws the account. A wallet that is not verified cannot receive rTokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy, hold and trade">
    The same credential works for any rToken and any integrated app, so you do not repeat onboarding for each asset.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Jurisdiction rules

Eligibility depends on where you live and how the series is offered. These rules are enforced by the token, not by policy alone.

| Pool | Admitted investors | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reg S pool | Non-US persons | Distribution-compliance period enforced on-chain, no flow-back to US persons during that period |
| Reg D pool | Accredited US investors | 12-month lock-up enforced per lot by the hook |
| Other | Depends on the series and the licensed venue entity | Shown on the listing |

<Warning>
  Availability differs by country and by asset. A listing is only open to you if your attestation satisfies that series' rules.
</Warning>

## Your credentials stay yours

Only a hashed reference sits on-chain in your attestation. The identity map linking your wallet to your verified identity is kept off-chain and encrypted by a Registrar, which keeps the legal register complete while respecting UK and EU data protection rules.

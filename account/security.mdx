## Turn on two-factor authentication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open security settings">
    Select your profile, then **Settings** and **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication">
    Follow the prompts to link an authenticator app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    Store them somewhere safe and offline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you lose access to your authenticator and your recovery codes, you may be locked out of your account. Keep both safe.
</Warning>

---
title: "Create an account"
description: "Sign up for Rivestor and confirm your email."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [rivestor.com](https://rivestor.com) and select **Sign up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Provide your email address and create a password. Use a password you do not use anywhere else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    Open the message we send you and select the confirmation link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your account is created. Next, [verify your identity](/account/verification).
</Check>

<Accordion title="I did not receive the confirmation email">
  Check your spam folder first. If the message is not there, request a new one from the sign-in page or [contact support](/resources/support).
</Accordion>

---
title: "Dual-engine architecture"
description: "Engine A tokenizes off-chain assets. Engine B wraps tokens that already exist. Both end in one rToken."
---

Rivestor lists assets through two complementary engines that terminate in the same token standard.

| Dimension | Engine A: Direct Asset Tokenization (SPV Factory) | Engine B: 1:1 Non-Custodial Token Wrapping (RTCS Vault) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who it serves | Owners of off-chain assets (real estate, private credit, commodities, private business equity) that are not yet tokenized | Issuers whose RWA token already exists but is trapped on a closed venue |
| Legal basis | Rivestor-standard structure: bankruptcy-remote SPV series, Golden Share, title protection package | The issuer's own legal structure, plus one of three integration tiers. An integration, not a new legal structure |
| Rivestor's role | Structuring technology, issuance platform and venue. Never the owner of the asset | Technology integrator. Never a nominee or custodian of the originals |
| rToken mint authority | Issuance program PDA, released only with Security Trustee co-signature | Vault program PDA, mints only against a verified deposit and burns on withdrawal |
| Compliance rules | The RTCS rule-set for the series | The union of the RTCS rule-set and the issuer's own eligibility rules |
| Stage | Pilot in Stage 1, full factory in Stage 2 | Starts in Stage 1, where assets are already on-chain |

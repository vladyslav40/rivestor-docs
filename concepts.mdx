A short glossary to keep the rest of the docs easy to follow.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Account" icon="user">
    Your identity on Rivestor. It holds your profile, verification status, and security settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Portfolio" icon="briefcase">
    A group of investments you manage together toward one goal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deposit" icon="arrow-down-to-line">
    Money you move from a linked funding source into Rivestor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Withdrawal" icon="arrow-up-from-line">
    Money you move from Rivestor back to a linked funding source.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is identity verification?">
    A required check that confirms who you are before you can invest. Learn more in [Identity verification](/account/verification).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is an API key?">
    A secret token that lets your own software act on your account. Treat it like a password. See [Authentication](/api/authentication).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

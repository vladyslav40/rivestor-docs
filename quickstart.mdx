This guide takes you through the full path: account, verification, funding, and your first investment.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you need:

- A valid email address
- A government-issued ID for identity verification
- A bank account or other funding source supported by Rivestor {/* TODO: confirm supported funding methods */}

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your email and choose a strong password. Confirm your email address from the message we send you.

    See [Create an account](/account/create-account) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">
    Submit your ID and the personal details we ask for. You can browse the platform while the review is in progress.

    See [Identity verification](/account/verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure your account">
    Turn on two-factor authentication before you add funds.

    <Warning>
      Never share your verification codes or password with anyone. Rivestor staff never ask for them.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add funds">
    Link a funding source and make a deposit. See [Deposits and withdrawals](/investing/deposits-withdrawals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first investment">
    Create a portfolio and allocate your funds. See [Portfolios](/investing/portfolios).
  </Step>
</Steps>

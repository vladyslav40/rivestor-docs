---
title: "Listing tracks"
description: "Three tracks size the legal protection package to the risk of the asset class."
---

A traditional SPV per asset typically costs thousands of dollars to form and takes weeks, which makes small assets uneconomic to list. The SPV Factory uses statutory series and cell structures so structuring cost scales with the risk of the asset. Applying institutional-grade structuring to a small receivables pool would make it unlistable, while applying light structuring to a large property would not protect lenders.

| Parameter | Express | Standard | Institutional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typical assets | Private-credit notes, receivables and invoice pools, warehouse-receipt commodities, small pools | Small or single-tenant real estate, SME business equity | Commercial real estate, leveraged or multi-tenant assets (flagship case) |
| Typical raise | $100k to $1M | $1M to $3M | $3M and above |
| Vehicle | Series of the Master LLC (or cell) issuing interests or notes directly | Series holding the asset directly where local title practice allows | Series owning a single-asset subsidiary LLC with an independent director |
| Protection package | Statutory series shield plus a perfected security interest (UCC-1 or local equivalent) or a pledge of warehouse receipts to the Security Trustee | Title restriction, charge under the Investor Protection Deed, Lockbox | Full package: subsidiary isolation, Golden Share, restriction, first-ranking charge, Lockbox, separateness covenants |
| Trustee | One programme trustee covers every Express series | Programme trustee with a Golden Share per series | Dedicated trustee per asset |
| Time to listing | 3 to 5 business days | 1 to 2 weeks | 3 to 4 weeks |

<Note>
  Track assignment is decided by the underwriting rules, not by the issuer. Third-party costs such as valuation, title or collateral checks, legal due diligence, registrations and trustee onboarding are borne by the issuer and are separate from Rivestor's fees. See [Fees](/issuers/fees). Contact [vs@rivestor.com](mailto:vs@rivestor.com) for a quote.
</Note>

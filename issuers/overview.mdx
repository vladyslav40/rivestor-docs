Rivestor is a distribution layer for issuers. You bring the asset or the token. Rivestor provides the legal structure (if needed), the compliance rules, the venue and the investor base.

## Two ways to list

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Direct tokenization (Engine A)" icon="factory" href="/issuers/direct-tokenization">
    For owners of off-chain assets that are not yet tokenized. The SPV Factory creates a bankruptcy-remote series and mints the rToken.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Token wrapping (Engine B)" icon="package" href="/issuers/token-wrapping">
    For issuers whose RWA token already exists. Lock it 1:1 in an RTCS vault and list the rToken, with no change to your corporate procedures at the lighter tiers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What you get

| Need | How Rivestor addresses it |
| --- | --- |
| Distribution | Eligible investors discover and compare assets in one venue after a single KYC. |
| Secondary market | A shared order book with atomic settlement. |
| Compliance | KYC/AML, whitelists, jurisdiction and holder limits enforced by the token. |
| Servicing | Income routed automatically to holders by the Distribution program. |
| Demand data | Which assets investors want, at what size and at what yield. |

## What you keep

<Check>
  Rivestor is never the owner of a natively issued asset and never a nominee or custodian of a wrapped token. Wrapped originals sit in a program-owned vault.
</Check>
